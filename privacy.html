Privacy Policy

Astral · Last updated October 8, 2026

Astral is a space-exploration app that displays public data and imagery from NASA and other open space-data providers. This policy explains what information the app handles. The short version: Astral itself does not collect, sell, or share your personal information. It shows ads from Google, whose software may collect some device data.

Information we collect

Astral has no servers, no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics. We receive no information about you. Google's ad software is the one exception, described under Advertising below.

Advertising

  • Astral shows ads served by Google AdMob. Astral asks Google for non-personalized ads and does not ask to track you.
  • Google's ad software may still collect device and usage data, such as your IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostics. Google uses it to serve, measure, and protect ads. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Users in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland are asked for consent. You can change your choice under Settings > Privacy Options.
  • Remove Ads is a purchase processed by Apple. Astral receives only whether you bought it.

Information stored on your device

The following is stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Your NASA API key (optional). If you choose to add one, it is saved securely in the iOS Keychain on your device and is sent only to NASA's own API endpoints to authenticate your requests.
  • App preferences, such as whether you have seen the welcome screen. These are stored in local app storage on your device.

Deleting the app removes all of this data.

Third-party data providers

To show you space data and imagery, Astral makes requests directly from your device to public APIs operated by third parties. As with any internet request, these providers may receive standard technical information such as your device's IP address. Astral does not add any identifying information to these requests. The providers are:

  • NASA Open APIs (APOD, asteroids, space weather)
  • NASA Exoplanet Archive
  • NASA Mars Rover Photos
  • NASA EPIC / DSCOVR
  • NASA EONET
  • NASA Image and Video Library
  • The Space Devs: Launch Library 2
  • Where The ISS At?

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy. Astral is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by NASA or any other provider.

Children's privacy

Astral is suitable for all ages, and it asks Google only for ads rated for general audiences. Astral itself does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
