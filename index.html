Frequently asked questions

Do I need a NASA API key?
No. Astral works out of the box. Adding your own free key (from api.nasa.gov) in Settings just raises the hourly request limit so data loads more reliably.
Why do I sometimes see "rate limit reached"?
Without a personal key, the app shares NASA's public demo key, which allows about 30 requests per hour. Add a free key in Settings to remove that limit. The limit resets automatically after about an hour.
Does Astral collect my data?
Astral itself does not. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Astral shows non-personalized ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect device and usage data to serve them. A one-time Remove Ads purchase turns them off. See the Privacy Policy.
Is Astral affiliated with NASA?
No. Astral is an independent app. All data comes from public APIs, credited in Settings > Data Sources. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.
